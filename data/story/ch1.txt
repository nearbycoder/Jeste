; ======================================================================
; CHAPTER 1 - Lantern Town
; An abandoned town where every lantern still burns. Mira's troupe once
; performed here; she meets Tobi, a nervous painter.
; ======================================================================

@ch1_arrive
!wait 0.5
mira:surprised: Huh. A whole town, halfway up the mountain.
mira:normal: Every lantern's lit... but there's nobody here.
mira:grin: Hellooo? Anyone want to see a juggling act? Front row seats! Free of charge!
!wait 0.6
mira:normal: ...
mira:worried: Tough crowd.
mira:normal: (Wait. I know this street. The Lark & Lantern troupe played here when I was little. Nana did her famous "three-ball sneeze".)
mira:grin: (Ha. I dropped every single ball that night. Nana told everyone it was part of the act.)

@ch1_poster
narrator:normal: A faded poster is pasted to the theater wall: "THE LARK & LANTERN TROUPE! Starring ODILE THE MAGNIFICENT and her granddaughter, LITTLE MIRA!"
mira:sad: ...Nana.
mira:grin: Look at that hair. I look like a startled chicken.
mira:normal: (Keep moving. Keep smiling. That's the job.)

@ch1_dressing
narrator:normal: A tiny dressing room hides behind the stage. A mirror ringed with dead bulbs. A chair. And on the table, something small and golden, ringing softly.
mira:surprised: That's... one of Nana's bells. From her cap! She lost them on tour, years ago.
mira:sad: How did it end up all the way up here?

@ch1_tobi
tobi:scared: AH! Don't - don't move! Please!
mira:surprised: Wha - what? Is there a bear?!
tobi:normal: No, no, you're just... standing exactly where my painting's light was. Sorry. Sorry! Hi.
tobi:happy: I'm Tobi. I paint. Or, I'm trying to. I'm supposed to paint the summit of Jeste for my first gallery show.
mira:grin: Mira! I juggle. Or, I'm trying to. I'm supposed to be a famous jester by now.
tobi:happy: Ha! Okay. Hi, Mira.
tobi:normal: I'm going to set up camp a little further up. There's a nice spot by the old well. Come warm up, if you want?
mira:happy: Free fire and a captive audience? I'll be there!

@ch1_end
!wait 0.4
tobi:happy: You made it! Here, sit. The fire's nice.
mira:grin: So then the bear says, "That's not my hat, that's my CUB!"
tobi:happy: Pfff - ha ha! Okay, okay, that's... that one's good.
mira:grin: And THEN -
tobi:normal: Mira.
mira:normal: Yeah?
tobi:normal: You've been telling jokes for an hour. Do you ever... stop? Being "on", I mean?
mira:grin: Stop? Ha! Why would I stop? Stopping is boring!
mira:normal: ...
tobi:sad: Sorry. That was a weird thing to ask. My mom always says I ask weird things.
mira:normal: No, it's fine. I'm just... tired, I guess. It's late.
mira:normal: (The fire crackles. Somewhere far below, a bell rings. I don't want to sleep tonight.)
!fade_out
